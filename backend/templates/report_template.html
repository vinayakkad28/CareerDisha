How to Read This Report

What is RIASEC?

RIASEC (also called Holland Codes) is a scientifically validated career interest model developed by psychologist John Holland. It categorizes interests into six types: Realistic, Investigative, Artistic, Social, Enterprising, and Conventional. Your unique combination of these interests points to career paths where you're most likely to thrive and find satisfaction.

Your Holland Code

Your Holland Code is a 3-letter combination of your top three RIASEC types. For example, "IRS" means your strongest interests are Investigative, then Realistic, then Social. This code is used to match you with careers that align with your natural interests.

Career Matches

We've matched your interest profile against 100+ career paths in the Indian education system. Each career recommendation includes education pathways, entrance exams, top colleges, salary expectations, and growth outlook — all specific to India.

For Parents (अभिभावकों के लिए)

The last section is specifically designed for parents, with clear recommendations and action items in both English and Hindi. It addresses common concerns about job security, salary, and career viability.

Important Disclaimer

This report is based on a career interest inventory, not a diagnostic assessment. It reflects your current interests, which are developing and may change over time — this is completely normal, especially for younger students. Interests do not equal aptitude; this report should be used as one input among many in career decision-making, alongside academic performance, personal goals, and family discussions. Career recommendations are generated using AI and checked by automated quality validation. Names, figures and dates should be verified against official sources before you act on them.
