How to Read This Report

What is RIASEC?

RIASEC (also called Holland Codes) is a scientifically validated career interest model developed by psychologist John Holland. It categorizes interests into six types: Realistic, Investigative, Artistic, Social, Enterprising, and Conventional. Your unique combination of these interests points to career paths where you're most likely to thrive and find satisfaction.

Your Holland Code

Your Holland Code is a 3-letter combination of your top three RIASEC types. For example, "IRS" means your strongest interests are Investigative, then Realistic, then Social. This code is used to match you with careers that align with your natural interests.

Career Matches

We've matched your interest profile against 100+ career paths in the Indian education system. Each career recommendation includes education pathways, entrance exams, top colleges, salary expectations, and growth outlook — all specific to India.
